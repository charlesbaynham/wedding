## Finding the cenote

It's a private cenote, so don't worry if the pin doesn't look like much on Google Maps — that's it! From Casa Tattva it's about 5 minutes on the highway plus 5–7 minutes on an unpaved track: roughly km 28.7, opposite the wells area (zona de pozos). Look for a tyre by the road, pass three wells, and the entrance is at the next tyre.

There's no phone signal or internet at the cenote, so please save this page (or a screenshot of the map / directions) before you set out.
