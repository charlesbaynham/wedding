**Date:** Sunday 25th October 2026
**Place:** near Puerto Morelos / Cancún, Quintana Roo, México

We would be honoured if you would join us for our wedding in October, in Mexico. The date is the 25th October, and we will be organising a few fun activities from Wednesday the 21st for those who are around earlier. We'll then be off for a mini-honeymoon, but Yucatán is beautiful so we hope you can take some time to explore it properly.

If you are coming (wonderful!) then the most important thing is to book flights ASAP! Flying to Cancún airport is the cheapest and quickest way to get to the wedding, and flights are at their cheapest right now, but the prices will go up.

If you are coming, please [fill in your details](/pr/26/activities.md) so we can plan the activities and transport — and take a look at the [full timetable](/pr/26/timetable.md).
