# Ceremony

[Home](/pr/29/index.md) · HTML version: https://wedding.houseabsolute.co.uk/pr/29/ceremony/

We are having a Mayan wedding at the beautiful [Cenote Agua Verde](https://what3words.com/grapples.firebird.heads) and we are thrilled that you can join us. The ceremony starts at **4:30 PM** — please arrive by 3:45 PM. See the [timetable](/pr/29/timetable.md) for times and directions.

Afterwards we move on to the reception at [Casa Tattva](https://maps.app.goo.gl/WEHwz3ng65J16pev8), a few minutes down the road. The whole day will be relaxed and informal.

## Dress code

We do have one special request: **please wear white / cream / linen!** It will be up to 30 °C so Charles will be in a short-sleeved linen shirt, Gaby in a floral dress of some sort. If you're looking for ideas, we recommend a [Guayabera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guayabera) for men — they're fantastic for everyday use too.

## Gifts

Absolutely none please. You have all travelled very far to be here with us — we are already favoured with the greatest gift we could wish for.
