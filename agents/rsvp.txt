# RSVP

[Home]({{ '/index.md' | relative_url }}) · HTML version: {{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/rsvp/

The RSVP form must be submitted by a human in a browser at {{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/rsvp/ (it uses reCAPTCHA). It asks for:

- Names of all guests
- Email
- Phone number
- Can you come? (Yes / No)
- Would you like transport to / from Puerto Morelos on the day of the wedding? (Yes / No / Don't know)
- Dietary requirements or allergies
- Any other comments

Guests planning to come should also fill in the [activities form]({{ '/activities.md' | relative_url }}).
