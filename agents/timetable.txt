## Wednesday 21 Oct — Welcome drinks & early birds luau

Casa Brisa y Mar, Puerto Morelos beach — from 5:00 PM. [Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aHXQmrGhYBZx2qBf7)

BYOB · bring your own drinks.

## Thursday 22 Oct — Xel-Há day trip

A full day at the water park. Please buy your own tickets before the day, at [xelha.com](https://www.xelha.com/en/) or a reseller such as booking.com, which is often cheaper. Everyone makes their own way there.

## Friday 23 Oct — Scuba & snorkelling

A private trip on the Puerto Morelos reef with Scuba Public Diving, for certified divers, beginners and snorkellers. Meet at 10:00 AM. Group rates from $550 MXN per person. [Prices & details]({{ '/diving.md' | relative_url }})

## Friday 23 Oct — Welcome party at sunset

The official start of the celebrations: tacos, a DJ and our own area on the beach at Ojo de Agua Beach Club, Puerto Morelos, 5:00–9:00 PM. Tacos included, paid bar. [Details]({{ '/welcome-party.md' | relative_url }})

## Saturday 24 Oct — Chichén Itzá trip

A guided day trip to the great pyramid with Aleman Travel Agency: air-conditioned transport, a bilingual guide, entry, a cenote swim, a Yucatecan buffet lunch and a stop in Valladolid. Charles & Gaby won't be joining this one (wedding prep!). You pay the agency directly.

$95 USD per person, all inclusive.
