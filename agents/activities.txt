# Activities form

[Home]({{ '/index.md' | relative_url }}) · HTML version: {{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/activities/

On the HTML site this is an interactive, step-by-step form (JavaScript and reCAPTCHA required), so it must be filled in by a human in a browser at {{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/activities/. If you fill it in again, your latest answers replace the earlier ones. This page lists what it asks so you can help someone prepare their answers.

## Questions

1. How many people are you filling this in for, and their names.
2. Your email — so we can reach you about bookings and transport.
3. When do you land in México? (Roughly is fine — helps us plan the early activities.)
4. Where are you staying? (Hotel, AirBnB, or town — so we can plan pick-ups.)
5. For each person, which activities they'd like to join:
   - **Welcome drinks & early-birds luau** — Wed 21 Oct, 5:00 PM, Casa Brisa y Mar, Puerto Morelos beach. BYOB.
   - **Xel-Há day trip** — Thu 22 Oct, full day. A natural water park: snorkel the inlet, float the lazy river and swim in cenotes. Food and drinks included in the ticket. Book your own tickets.
   - **Scuba & snorkelling** — Fri 23 Oct. The Puerto Morelos reef, part of the second-largest barrier reef in the world. Each person joining picks their level (certified diver, beginner, snorkeller); see [diving & snorkelling]({{ '/diving.md' | relative_url }}) for options and prices.
   - **Welcome party at sunset** — Fri 23 Oct, 5:00 PM, Puerto Morelos beach. Free entry, paid bar. See [welcome party]({{ '/welcome-party.md' | relative_url }}).
   - **Chichén Itzá trip** — Sat 24 Oct, full day. Charles & Gaby won't make this one (wedding prep!) but will help the group organise it. ≈ $40 USD per person (to be confirmed).
6. For each person, whether they'd like shared transport on the wedding day (Sun 25 Oct):
   - Puerto Morelos → Cenote Agua Verde (to the ceremony)
   - Cenote Agua Verde → Casa Tattva (on to the reception)
   - Casa Tattva → Puerto Morelos (home at the end of the night)
7. Review and submit.

See the [timetable]({{ '/timetable.md' | relative_url }}) for the full schedule.
