# RSVP

[Home](/pr/30/index.md) · HTML version: https://wedding.houseabsolute.co.uk/pr/30/rsvp/

The RSVP form must be submitted by a human in a browser at https://wedding.houseabsolute.co.uk/pr/30/rsvp/ (it uses reCAPTCHA). It asks for:

- Names of all guests
- Email
- Phone number
- Can you come? (Yes / No)
- Would you like transport to / from Puerto Morelos on the day of the wedding? (Yes / No / Don't know)
- Dietary requirements or allergies
- Any other comments
