## Friday 23 Oct · 5:00 – 9:00 PM — Ojo de Agua Beach Club, Puerto Morelos

This is the official start of the celebrations. We have our own reserved area on the beach with sun loungers and Bali beds, and a DJ all evening. Come whenever you like between 5 and 9.

[Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ojo+de+Agua+Beach+Club+Puerto+Morelos)

## Food is on us

A taquiza: arrachera (grilled steak) and chicken al pastor tacos, with salsas, onion, coriander and lime.

## Drinks

Buy your own drinks at the club's bar. It's a beach club, so its rules don't allow outside food or drink. Please don't bring your own. Tips aren't included.

Free entry · tacos included · pay-as-you-go bar.

## Let us know you're coming

So we can get the numbers right, please let us know if you're coming on the [activities form](/pr/26/activities.md). If you fill it in again, your latest answers replace the earlier ones.
